# Front-end Style Guide

## Layout

The designs were created to the following widths:

- Mobile: 375px
- Desktop: 1440px

> 💡 These are just the design sizes. Ensure content is responsive and meets WCAG requirements by testing the full range of screen sizes from 320px to large screens.

---

## Colors

### Base / Background

- Charcoal Black: #121212

### Primary Accent

- Warm Terracotta: #cd7866

### Secondary Accent

- Gold Highlight: #f6b162

### Neutral / Supporting

- Deep Rose: #8b5160
- Neutral Text: #f2ecff

### Usage Notes

- Base/background: hero sections, full-page background, section backgrounds  
- Primary accent: main buttons, CTA highlights, hover effects  
- Secondary accent: icons, smaller buttons, hover effects  
- Neutral text: paragraph text, menu descriptions, general content  
- Supporting tone: secondary buttons, small details, overlays

---

## Typography

### Body Copy

- Font size (paragraph): 16px
- Line height: 1.5

### Headings

- h1 (Hero): 48–60px, line-height 1.2
- h2 (Section): 32–40px, line-height 1.3
- h3 (Subsection / Dish Name): 24–28px, line-height 1.3
- Buttons: 16–18px, bold or semi-bold
- Captions / Small labels: 12–14px, line-height 1.2

### Font

- Family: [Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) for headings / buttons  
- Family: [Poppins](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins) for body copy and captions  
- Weights: 400(noraml/regular), 500(medium), 600(semi-bold), 700(bold)

### Styling Notes

- Letter spacing: 0.5–1px for headings  
- Text transform: buttons uppercase small-caps, headings title-case  
- Contrast: headings/buttons in accent colors (#cd7866, #f6b162), paragraphs in #f2ecff  

---

## Buttons

- Primary CTA: background #cd7866, text #121212  
- Secondary / small buttons: background #f6b162, text #121212  
- Hover states: slightly darken background or add subtle shadow

---

## Notes

- Ensure consistent spacing and alignment across sections.  
- Always check readability of text over images — add overlays if necessary.  
- All images should be optimized for web and responsive.  
- Keep the Fusion + Warm & Luxurious tone in mind when adjusting layout or colors.
